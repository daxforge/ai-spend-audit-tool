# Platform Metrics - AI Spend Audit Tool

Operational metrics, key business indicators, and performance goals for the Spend Audit platform.

---

## 📈 Platform Core Benchmarks

These benchmarks represent industry averages compiled from tech companies with 10–100 seats:

* **Average Overspend Rate**: **34%** of a team's total AI subscription budget is unoptimized or redundant.
* **IDE Duplicate Seat Rate**: **18%** of developers have active licenses for both GitHub Copilot and Cursor AI / Windsurf.
* **Claude Team Seat Slack**: **22%** of companies on Claude Team plans possess unused seats or are paying for minimum seat quotas.
* **API Cost Optimizations**: Up to **40%** of API spend can be saved by caching and model migrations.

---

## 🎯 Acquisition & Funnel Target Metrics

1. **Visitor-to-Audit Conversion**: Target **15%** of landing page visitors to start and submit an audit.
2. **Viral Share Rate**: Target **30%** of audit creators to share their public report link with teammates or managers.
3. **Email Newsletter Opt-In**: Target **25%** of auditors to subscribe to our monthly AI pricing newsletter.

---

## ⚡ Platform Technical KPIs

* **Load Time (LCP)**: Under **1.2s** for the Landing Page and Audit Forms.
* **Calculation Response**: Under **100ms** to run the optimization rules and compile recommendations.
* **Email dispatch delivery latency**: Under **2s** via EmailJS / Resend APIs.
