# User Interviews & Case Studies - AI Spend Audit Tool

Simulated user interviews highlighting SaaS spend pain points and feedback from initial adopters.

---

## 🎙️ Interview 1: Technical Leader (CTO)
* **Company**: Tech startup, 35 employees.
* **Key Challenge**: Redundant coding assistants.
* **Interview Transcript Highlight**:
  > *"We standardized on Cursor AI in January. Two weeks ago, I ran this audit and discovered 12 developers were still expensing GitHub Copilot. Our expensing software doesn't flag that because the descriptions are different. Canceling those 12 Copilot licenses saved us $228/month instantly. That's $2,700 a year we were just throwing away."*

---

## 🎙️ Interview 2: Finance Ops Manager
* **Company**: Digital agency, 60 employees.
* **Key Challenge**: Seat minimum minimum fees.
* **Interview Transcript Highlight**:
  > *"We set up Claude Team licenses because we wanted our engineering team to collaborate on prompts. We only had 3 users but had to pay for the 5-seat minimum ($150/mo). The audit flagged that we could just downgrade to 3 individual Claude Pro accounts for $20 each, saving $90/mo. We made the switch immediately."*

---

## 🎙️ Interview 3: VP of AI Operations
* **Company**: AI SaaS platform, 120 employees.
* **Key Challenge**: Bloated developer API endpoints.
* **Interview Transcript Highlight**:
  > *"Our OpenAI API bill was climbing past $3,000/mo. The audit suggested two things: move 80% of our background text classifications from GPT-4o to GPT-4o-mini, and turn on semantic caching. Implementing those changes cut our monthly bill to $1,100. That's a 63% cost reduction with no drop in system accuracy."*
