# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy - AI Spend Audit Tool

Launch roadmap and distribution strategies for acquiring users and customers.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience (ICP)

Our Ideal Customer Profile consists of technical and operational leaders at companies with 10 to 150 employees:

1. **CTOs & VPs of Engineering**: Responsible for developer tooling budgets. Fatigued by developers expensing multiple coding assistants (e.g. Cursor, Windsurf, Copilot) simultaneously.
2. **CFOs & Finance Managers**: Tasked with auditing SaaS budgets. Frustrated by personal expense reports for AI subscriptions lacking admin controls.
3. **Engineering Managers**: Want to ensure their engineers have access to premium tooling without wasting budget.

---

## 🚀 Growth Loops (Viral Mechanics)

The application has a built-in virality mechanic:

```text
User Submits Audit Form 
  ➜ Generates Results Dashboard 
    ➜ Shares Public Report link with CFO/CTO 
      ➜ Stakeholder views report 
        ➜ Clicks "Audit Your Stack" CTA 
          ➜ Loop repeats
```

* **Viral Shared Reports**: Every generated audit report has a shareable link. When shared with colleagues or management, the page features a prominent CTA: *"Audit Your Own Team Stack"*.

---

## 📣 Launch & Distribution Channels

### 1. Developer Communities (Organic)
* **Product Hunt**: Launch as a free utility. Offer a free downloadable optimization template.
* **Hacker News**: Submit as a "Show HN" project. Frame it around developer SaaS pricing analysis.
* **X/Twitter**: Target the "build in public" dev community. Share real examples of seat redundancies (Cursor + Copilot).

### 2. Cold Outreach (B2B Sales)
* **Startups with 20-80 staff**: Pull lists of tech companies recently funded. Reach out to their CTOs with a simple pitch: *"Are your engineers expensing both Cursor and GitHub Copilot? Run this 2-minute free audit."*
